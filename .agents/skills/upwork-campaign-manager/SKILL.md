# 🚀 Upwork Campaign Manager Skill

This skill governs the complete workflow for evaluating Upwork job postings, filtering leads by speed and quality, performing critical reviews, and generating high-converting proposals with mandatory milestone breakdowns.

---

## 📌 Mandatory Milestone Rule (ALWAYS INCLUDE)

Every proposal generated (Fixed-Price or Hourly) MUST explicitly include a **Production Milestone Roadmap**:

- **Milestone 1:** `Requirements Review, Architecture & 24-Hour $100 Paid Trial` — **$100.00**
- **Milestone 2:** `Core Development & System Integration` — **50% of remaining scope**
- **Milestone 3:** `Testing, Deployment & Handover` — **Final 50% of remaining scope**

---

## 🧐 Critical Assessment Rules (Mandatory Review)

For every evaluated job posting, perform an adversarial, unvarnished critical review covering:

1. **Win Rate Estimate:** Evaluate submission window (<5 proposals vs >20), client total spent, and budget alignment.
2. **Red Flags & Risks:** Surface scope creep risks, vague specs, unrealistic expectations, or unverified payment status.
3. **Connects ROI Efficiency:** Determine if spending 7-20 Connects yields high conversion or 5-star review potential.
4. **Final Verdict:**
   - **`🔥 HIGH PRIORITY (Submit Immediately)`**
   - **`⚡ MEDIUM MATCH (Submit with $100 Paid PoC)`**
   - **`🛑 SKIP (Save Connects)`**
