# 🗣️ Voice AI Sales Playbook & Objection Engine Skill

This skill defines the structured decision tree and counter-argument playbook for Voice AI agents handling sales objections.

---

## 🛠️ Core Objection Counter-Arguments

1. **Objection: "It's too expensive / What is the pricing?"**
   - **AI Counter:** "I completely understand. Our clients typically save 15-20 hours a week on manual call handling, and booking just 2 additional appointments covers the entire monthly system cost. Let's start with a quick 24-hour test so you can see the live results first."

2. **Objection: "I need to think about it / Send me an email."**
   - **AI Counter:** "Fair enough! I can definitely send over the overview. However, our calendar slots for this week fill up quickly. Can I hold a tentative 10-minute slot for tomorrow afternoon while you review the info?"

3. **Objection: "Can an AI really handle our specific dealership inventory?"**
   - **AI Counter:** "Great question. Our AI reads directly from your live inventory database in real time. It never guesses or invents stock numbers—if a vehicle is available, it provides exact specs; if sold, it suggests live alternatives."
